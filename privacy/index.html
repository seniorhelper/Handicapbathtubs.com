Privacy note

Last updated October 15, 2026.

HandicapBathtubs.com is operated by Aging Safely Baths. This note explains what happens to information you share with us through this website.

What we collect

When you send the quote form, we receive the details you type in: your name, phone number, ZIP or postal code, email address if you give one, your answers about mobility and bathing help, any notes, and the page you sent the form from. We do not ask for health records, and you do not need to share a diagnosis to get a quote.

How it is sent

The form is delivered by FormSubmit, an email forwarding service, to the Aging Safely Baths sales inbox. FormSubmit handles the message in transit under its own privacy policy.

How we use it

Only to answer your request: to call or email you about tubs, showers, installation, delivery or funding paperwork. We do not sell or rent your information.

Cookies and tracking

This site does not set its own cookies or run advertising trackers. The BBB seal image is loaded from the Better Business Bureau's server.

Your choices

To ask what we hold about you, or to have it deleted, email sales@agingsafelybaths.com or call 1-888-779-2284.
